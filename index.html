Common questions

PeakReel can't see my videos

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, choose PeakReel, then Photos, and allow access to the videos you want to use. With limited access, PeakReel shows only the videos you've chosen.

Why is a video downloading first?

Videos stored in iCloud are downloaded before they can be used. A stable Wi-Fi connection helps with long videos.

Can I edit the whole video, not only the best moments?

Yes. In the editor, switch from Best Moments to Full. Your video is used whole, split scene by scene. You can also add any part of a video as a new moment.

Why can't I use a song from Apple Music?

Songs from Apple Music are copy-protected. Choose one of the included songs, or an audio file you own.

Subtitles aren't available in my language

Subtitles are made on your iPhone, with the languages it supports. You can choose the spoken language in the Subtitles tool.

How do I delete saved reels?

Open Settings in PeakReel, then Saved Reels. Deleting a reel there keeps the copy in your photo library.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
